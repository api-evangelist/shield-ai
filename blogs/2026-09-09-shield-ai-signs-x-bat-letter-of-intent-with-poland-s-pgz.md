KIELCE, Poland (September 9, 2026) — At a signing event yesterday at MSPO, the International Defence Industry Exhibition, Shield AI, Polska Grupa Zbrojeniowa S.A. (PGZ) and Wojskowe Zakłady Lotnicze nr 2 S.A. (WZL-2) signed a letter of intent to partner on the design, production and delivery of X-BAT, and on sustaining it in Poland.
